## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The outings of "Que faire à Paris ?", the agenda of the City of Paris (open data, no account),
  downloaded every 8 hours.
- One device per zone: around the house located in Gladys, the whole agenda, or any of the 20
  arrondissements, with the number of events today, this weekend and in the next 7 days, and the
  next event (title, place, date).
- Filters: themes, keywords, free events only, events for children, exhibitions and long runs.
- "Outings in Paris" dashboard widget: the next events of the zones, with date, place, "Free" badge
  and link.
- Scene triggers "New event announced" and "Event starting today", and the scene action "What to do
  in Paris" (one sentence).
- Texts and documentation in English, French, German and Spanish.
